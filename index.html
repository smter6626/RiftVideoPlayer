Features

Built for cinematic playback.

Every detail of Rift is engineered for immersive, frame-perfect video playback on macOS: the render path, the control surface, and the way the interface disappears when the picture needs the room.

Liquid Glass UI

Adaptive transparency with native macOS blur. Controls float over your content with spring animations that feel like part of the system.

Frame+ AI Interpolation

Real-time motion interpolation via RIFE neural network. GPU-accelerated on Metal. 24 fps to 120 fps without artifacts.

Universal Format Support

Open MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, and more. FFmpeg-powered conversion handles anything your library throws at it.

HDR & Visual Enhancements

Adaptive tone-mapping, highlight control, sharpening. Core Image filters make HDR content look stunning on any display.

Movable Controls

Drag the control bar anywhere in the window. Double-tap resets to center. Auto-hides when you're immersed.

Native Metal Pipeline

Pure Metal rendering with hardware acceleration. Plays 4K, 8K, and high-bitrate content with zero-copy efficiency.
